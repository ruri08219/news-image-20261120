One image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026, as a
classic newspaper front page — **"THE WORLD IN A DAY"**.

## The stories on the page

- **Trump pushes Iran decision to 2027** — "See what happens after Jan 1," the president says
- **Voyager 1 passes one light-day from Earth** — NASA confirms the probe is now 24 light-hours away
- **Myanmar guilty of genocide, world court rules** — historic ICJ judgment in The Hague on the Rohingya
- **Cyclone Akvan: 300,000 evacuated in Odisha** — severe storm bears down on India's east coast
- **Europe ready for talks with Russia on Ukraine** — diplomats signal willingness to meet for ceasefire discussions
- **Kyiv drone raid damages two embassies** — Austrian and Dutch missions hit, staff unharmed
- **Hayabusa2 drops asteroid landing after engine trouble** — JAXA postpones the landing
- **GTA 6 sales pass $1.8 billion** — overtaking The Odyssey's global run

## How it works

`main.py` copies the pre-built front-page image (`entry.jpg`, 1024x1024)
byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`. It is fully deterministic and uses no
network access, so repeated runs produce identical bytes.
